## Overview

**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama television series created by **Vince Gilligan**. It aired from 2008 to 2013 and consists of five gripping seasons.

### 🧪 Plot Summary

A high school chemistry teacher, **Walter White**, turns to making methamphetamine after being diagnosed with terminal cancer. With the help of his former student **Jesse Pinkman**, Walter descends into the criminal underworld.

---

## Main Characters

- **Walter White** – aka *Heisenberg*
- **Jesse Pinkman**
- **Skyler White**
- **Hank Schrader**
- **Saul Goodman**
- **Gus Fring**
